# Donate

Acutis Firewall is free, has no ads, no account and no analytics. It is built and
maintained in spare time, and donations pay for test devices, blocklist hosting and the
hours spent keeping the app working on new Android releases.

Every contribution is optional. The app is complete and stays fully functional without
one.

Bitcoin is the only way to donate at the moment. There is no Lightning address and no
other currency.

## How to donate

1. Open a Bitcoin wallet. Any wallet that supports native SegWit (`bc1q…`) addresses
   works, including BlueWallet, Electrum, Sparrow, Muun and Bitcoin Core on desktop.
2. Scan the QR code above with the wallet's scanner, or copy the address and paste it
   into the wallet's "send to" field.
3. Send any amount. There is no minimum and no account, email or name is needed.
4. Confirm the address in the wallet before sending. It starts with `bc1q` and ends
   with `tu2quce`.

## Check a donation arrived

Donations are public on the Bitcoin blockchain. Paste the address into a block explorer
such as [mempool.space](https://mempool.space/address/bc1q408lpl7etcfwdvuxh84wvcwc6gvzwdktu2quce)
to see incoming payments. A transaction shows up there immediately, before it is
confirmed.

## Good to know

- **Mainnet only.** This is a real Bitcoin address, not a testnet one. Sending coins
  from another network (testnet, or an altcoin address that looks similar) loses them.
- **Fees apply.** Your wallet adds a network fee. A donation of a few dollars is fine;
  if the fee looks larger than the amount, wait for quieter network conditions.
- **Anonymous.** No email or identity is collected. A donation is visible on the public
  blockchain, but it is not linked to you by this project.
- **Not tax-deductible.** Donations are gifts, not payments for a product or service.
